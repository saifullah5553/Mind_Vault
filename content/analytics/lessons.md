# What the numbers say (read before choosing a topic and writing)

The daily run updates this file from `storage/analytics/latest.md`
(`.venv\Scripts\python -m scripts.analytics_report`). Each lesson names its evidence.
Under ~100 views a video, differences are mostly chance: keep a lesson only while it
keeps repeating, and mark it "tentative" until it does.

## 2026-11-13 (first week: 25 uploads, about 600 views, 1 subscriber)

1. **Shorts are the channel's front door.** 21 Shorts took 97% of all views; the four long
   videos took 13 views between them. Treat every reel as a film of its own: its first
   line and headline matter as much as the long video's title.
2. **Conflict, loss and reversal beat description (tentative, repeats 4 times).** Top
   Shorts: "How Iceland Lost Its Althing, Then Won It Back" (130 views, 8 likes, the best
   like rate), "The Night the North Sea Broke the Dutch Dikes" (130), "Why Your Hobby Died
   the Moment It Became Your Job" (73), "A 15-Year-Old Emperor and the Boshin War" (41).
   Bottom: "A Parliament Born in a Rift" (2), "Japan's Leaders Sail to See the West" (1),
   "Tsuda Umeko, Japan's First Railway and the Tomioka Silk Mill" (0), "The Maeslant
   Barrier" (6). Write reel headlines and titles around a turn: someone loses, breaks,
   is forced, fights back, wins it back. Lists of names ("X, Y and Z") do worst.
3. **A reel must stand on its own from its first word (tentative).** Parts that open
   mid-thought ("It was only the start", "They also hoped…") sit in the bottom third.
   Choose cut points where a new turn starts, and use `--parts` to move them.
4. **Part 1 averages 52 views, later parts 23.** The story's opening is the strongest
   reel; later parts need their own hook to survive without it.
5. **Long videos are not yet found by anyone.** Until retention data is available, keep
   the long video as the full story but put the effort into title + thumbnail curiosity
   ("Why…", "How… lost…", a number, a turn) and into reels that send viewers to it.

Missing: retention, average view percentage, shares and subscribers per video need the
YouTube Analytics API enabled in the Google Cloud project (owner action).
