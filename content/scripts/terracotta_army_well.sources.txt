Chinese farmers digging a well near Xi'an on March 29, 1974 made the find; Pit 1 held about 6,000 life-size terracotta statues; four pits, pit 2 cavalry and infantry, pit 3 high-ranking officers and chariots, pit 4 empty | https://www.history.com/articles/5-things-you-may-not-know-about-the-terra-cotta-army
Four main pits about 7 m deep lie about 1,500 m east of the burial mound; pit 4 is empty, perhaps left unfinished by its builders | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terracotta_Army
Pit 2 was discovered by drilling in May 1976 and pit 3 in July 1976 | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mausoleum_of_Qin_Shi_Huang
2007 estimate: more than 8,000 soldiers, 130 chariots with 520 horses and 150 cavalry horses; figures typically 1.75 m to about 2 m tall, officers typically taller | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terracotta_Army
Qin Shi Huang (Ying Zheng) was born in 259 BC in Handan; by 221 BC he had conquered all the other warring states and unified China, taking the title Shi Huangdi, first emperor | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qin_Shi_Huang
He died in 210 BC, aged 49, at Shaqiu during his fifth tour of eastern China | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qin_Shi_Huang
Work on the mausoleum began in 246 BC when he was aged 13 (the year he became king of Qin) and lasted from 246 to 208 BC; the tomb mound is 76 m tall and shaped like a truncated pyramid | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mausoleum_of_Qin_Shi_Huang
Qin Shi Huang, ruler from 221 to 210 BC, was the first emperor to unify China under a single dynasty and standardized the nation's writing system, currency and measurements | https://www.nationalgeographic.com/magazine/article/terra-cotta-warriors
Qin Shi Huang sent Xu Fu to the eastern seas to search for the elixir of life; Xu Fu never returned | https://www.history.com/articles/first-emperor-china-qin-shi-huang-xu-fu
Sima Qian (145-90 BC) wrote about the mausoleum a century after its completion in the Records of the Grand Historian (Shiji): 700,000 men were sent from all over the empire; mercury was used to simulate the hundred rivers and the great sea; craftsmen made crossbows and arrows primed to shoot anyone entering; he never mentioned the terracotta army | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mausoleum_of_Qin_Shi_Huang
Sima Qian's account does not mention the terracotta army; the work was done by some 700,000 workers over nearly three decades | https://www.history.com/articles/5-things-you-may-not-know-about-the-terra-cotta-army
Figures were made from moulds (only eight moulds for heads) with clay details added by hand so each soldier appears unique; scholars identified 10 base facial forms | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terracotta_Army
Some 40,000 bronze weapons were found, including battle axes, crossbows, arrowheads and spears; it was long claimed they were preserved by chrome plating | https://www.history.com/articles/5-things-you-may-not-know-about-the-terra-cotta-army
A 2019 study of 464 bronze weapons found the chromium came from nearby lacquer, not an anti-rust treatment; soil pH and small particle size, plus high tin content, likely explain the preservation | https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2019/apr/scientists-shed-light-preservation-mystery-terracotta-army-weapons
The figures were painted over lacquer; once exposed the lacquer can curl after 15 seconds and flake off in four minutes; workers spray exposed colour with protective solution and wrap it in plastic; Yang Rongrong has pieced statues together since 1974 and reassembled an army of a thousand warriors; burial complex covers 35 square miles; less than one percent of the tomb complex excavated | https://www.nationalgeographic.com/magazine/article/terra-cotta-warriors
Archaeologists were initially unable to preserve the coat of paint, which shed rapidly after excavation | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mausoleum_of_Qin_Shi_Huang
In 2005 a team led by Duan Qingbo tested 4,000 samples from the burial mound for mercury and all came back highly positive; less than 1 percent of the tomb has been excavated | https://www.history.com/articles/5-things-you-may-not-know-about-the-terra-cotta-army
In one mercury survey one measured point reached 1,440 ppb while the other 53 points averaged about 205 ppb (about seven times) | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mausoleum_of_Qin_Shi_Huang
The central tomb has not been excavated, possibly due to concerns over preservation of its artifacts | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terracotta_Army
